# Your privacy choices — AI Tamagotchi

**Last updated: 17 October 2026**

AI Tamagotchi collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no
tracking, no advertising and no crash reporting, so there is nothing about you
for the developer to hold, sell, share or profile.

That means most of the choices other apps offer you do not apply here — and the
ones that do are all under your direct control on your own device.

## Choices that do not apply

| Choice | Status |
|---|---|
| Opt out of sale or sharing of personal data | Nothing is collected, so nothing is sold or shared |
| Opt out of targeted advertising | The app contains no advertising and uses no advertising identifier |
| Opt out of analytics or profiling | The app contains no analytics |
| Withdraw consent to data processing | No personal data is processed off your device |
| Request a copy of your data | No data reaches the developer to copy |
| Request deletion of data held about you | No data reaches the developer to delete |

## Choices that do apply

Everything the app remembers lives in its own sandbox on your iPhone. You can
remove any of it yourself, without asking anyone:

- **Clear the conversation.** Open **Chat** and tap **[clear]** — or type
  `/clear` or `/reset`. The conversation history is discarded and is no longer
  used when your creature answers.
- **Delete a downloaded language model.** In the model list on the creation
  screen, remove any model you have downloaded. This frees the storage and
  removes the file from your device.
- **Delete a creature.** Use **creature --manage** in the app to replace the
  current creature. Export its DNA first if you want to keep the lineage.
- **Delete DNA files.** Any DNA you export is a plain JSON file that you chose
  where to save — delete it like any other file.
- **Delete everything.** Delete the app. Every piece of local data — creature,
  memories, conversations, model files — is deleted with it.

## Network connections you initiate

The app works with no network connection at all. The only network request it
ever makes is the **optional language model download** you start yourself, which
goes directly from Hugging Face to your device. If you never download a model,
the app makes no network requests whatsoever. While a download is running, your
IP address reaches Hugging Face, as with any web request; see
[Hugging Face's privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy).

## Your rights

If you are in the EU, UK, California or another region with data protection
rights, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to
processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory
authority. Because the app collects nothing, the developer holds no personal
data about you and there is no request to make — but if you believe otherwise,
write to **andrej.zwitter@gmail.com** and you will get an answer.
