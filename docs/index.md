title: AI Tamagotchi
---

An AI-powered digital pet that lives on your iPhone. A real language model runs
on-device — no cloud, no accounts, and nothing leaves your device.

**On the App Store (coming soon).**

## The app

- **On-device AI.** Conversations are generated by a language model running
  locally through `llama.rn`. No API keys, no servers, no data collection.
- **Memory that matters.** Your creature remembers how you treat it, and those
  memories shape how it talks to you.
- **DNA and evolution.** Every creature is procedurally generated and evolves
  through five stages across four branches.
- **A terminal aesthetic.** Amber phosphor on warm black, because pets deserve
  a command line.
